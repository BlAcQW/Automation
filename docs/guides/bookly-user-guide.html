1. Before you start

You will need:

  • A phone number for your business that is not already used on regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business on a phone. This becomes your Bookly number.
  • A Facebook/Meta business account (free) to connect WhatsApp.
  • A Paystack account if you want to collect deposits by mobile money or card. Optional, but strongly recommended.
  • An email address you can access, for verification and password resets.
Plan 30–45 minutes for first-time setup. The WhatsApp connection is the longest part; everything after that is quick.

2. Create your account

1 Go to bookly.ikieguy.online and choose Register.
2 Enter your name, business name, email and a password (at least 8 characters), then agree to the terms.
3 Check your email and click the verification link. You can keep using Bookly before verifying — you'll just see a reminder banner.
4 You're in. Every new account starts on a 14-day free Pro trial, so all features are unlocked while you set up.

Forgot your password later? On the login screen choose Forgot password, and we'll email you a reset link.

3. Connect WhatsApp

This is the most important step — until it's done, nothing can reach your customers.

1 Open WhatsApp in the menu.
2 Click Connect WhatsApp Business. A Facebook window opens.
3 Sign in to Facebook, pick (or create) your business, and choose the phone number you want to use.
4 Confirm the code Meta sends to that number.
5 Back in Bookly, the status turns Live. Send a test message to check.
If the Facebook window is blocked (ad-blocker, or it simply won't open), use Connect manually instead on the same page and paste the Phone Number ID, WhatsApp Business Account ID and access token from Meta → WhatsApp → API Setup.
Important: a number already active on the WhatsApp app on a phone cannot be used. Either use a fresh number, or delete the existing WhatsApp account on that number first.

4. Add your services

The assistant can only offer what you list here — it will never invent a service or a price.

1 Go to Services → Add service.
2 Fill in:
  • Name — what customers will see, e.g. "Haircut".
  • Price and Duration in minutes — duration controls how your day is split into slots.
  • Category (optional) — to group services.
  • Deposit (optional) — overrides your default deposit for this one service. Leave blank to use the default; set 0 for no deposit.
3 Keep Active on. Switch it off any time to hide a service without deleting it.
Get durations right — a 30-minute service and a 60-minute service produce completely different available times.

5. Set your working hours

1 Go to Availability.
2 Turn each day on or off, and set opening and closing times.
3 Press Save working hours.

Blackout dates

Closing for a holiday, a funeral, or travel? Add the date under Blackout dates with an optional reason. The assistant will not offer any time on those days.

Bookly only ever offers times that are genuinely free — it checks your hours, your blackout dates and your existing bookings before it says anything.

6. Get paid: deposits & Paystack

Deposits are the single biggest reason businesses stop losing money to no-shows. A customer who has paid something turns up.

Connect Paystack

1 Go to Settings and find the payments section.
2 Paste your Paystack secret key (from your Paystack dashboard).
3 Save. Customers can now pay by mobile money or card, and the money goes straight to your Paystack account — Bookly never holds your money.

Choose your deposit policy

SettingWhereWhat it does
Ask for a depositSettings → DepositsMaster switch. Off = every booking is confirmed immediately with no payment.
Deposit amountSettings → DepositsThe default amount asked for, in your account currency.
Per-service depositServices → edit a serviceOverrides the default for that service. Set 0 for a service that needs no deposit.

How a deposit booking behaves

  • The slot is held for 30 minutes, not confirmed. If the customer doesn't pay in that time, the slot is released for someone else.
  • The assistant asks whether they'd like to pay now or pay when they arrive.
  • Choose pay now → it sends a real Paystack link. Paying confirms the booking instantly.
  • Choose pay on arrival → the booking is confirmed and you collect on the day.
  • If a customer asks later "how do I pay?", the assistant finds their unpaid booking and re-sends the link.
If Paystack isn't connected, no payment link can be created. Bookly confirms the booking anyway and tells the customer to pay on arrival — and notifies you so nothing is lost silently.

7. Your day-to-day: the dashboard

Overview is your morning screen. It shows:

  • Total bookings, today's bookings, active chats and total customers.
  • Whether WhatsApp is Live or needs attention.
  • Recent activity, so you can see what happened overnight.

8. Bookings

The Bookings page lists every appointment. Use the filter chips to switch between All, Confirmed, Pending, Completed and Cancelled.

StatusMeaning
Pending paymentHeld, waiting for the deposit. Released if unpaid after 30 minutes.
ConfirmedLocked in. Deposit paid, or no deposit needed.
CompletedThe appointment happened.
CancelledCancelled by you or the customer.
No showThey didn't turn up.

Open any booking to see the customer, service, time and reference, message them on WhatsApp in one tap, resend a payment link, or cancel.

Adding a booking yourself

Walk-in or phone booking? Add it from the Bookings page. Bookings you create are confirmed straight away with no deposit — Bookly assumes you're handling payment in person. It still checks the slot is genuinely free.

9. Chats & taking over from the assistant

Chats is your WhatsApp inbox. Every customer conversation appears here.

  • A green dot means the customer spoke last and is waiting on a reply.
  • A badge shows who is handling it: Bot or You.
  • Open a chat and simply type to reply — the assistant steps aside automatically.
  • Press Resume bot to hand it back when you're done.

The assistant also hands over to you by itself when a customer asks for a person, sounds frustrated, or asks something outside bookings.

10. The 24-hour rule & message templates

This is a WhatsApp rule, not a Bookly limitation, and it surprises everyone at first.

You can only send free-typed messages within 24 hours of the customer's last message. Outside that window, WhatsApp only allows pre-approved templates.

What this means in practice:

  • While a customer is chatting with you, reply freely — no restriction.
  • A day later, you cannot just type "Hi, are you coming?". The message box will tell you the window has closed.
  • Reminders and confirmations still work, because Bookly sends those as approved templates.

Templates

The Templates page lists your Meta-approved message templates and whether each is Approved or Pending. You register templates in WhatsApp Manager; Bookly uses them for booking confirmations, reminders and cancellations.

If reminders aren't arriving, check here first — an unapproved or missing template is the usual cause.

12. Your team

Only the owner can manage the team. Go to Team (in More on mobile) to:

  • Add a member — name, email and a temporary password to share with them. They can log in and handle bookings and chats.
  • Switch someone off when they leave — this blocks access without deleting their history.

The owner account cannot be deactivated, and you cannot deactivate yourself.

15. What the assistant can and cannot do

It canIt will not
  • List your services with real prices
  • Offer only genuinely free times
  • Create, hold and confirm a booking
  • Look up a customer's own bookings
  • Send a real deposit payment link
  • Hand the chat over to you
  • Invent a service, price or free slot
  • Make up or alter a payment link
  • Double-book a taken slot
  • Offer times outside your hours or on a blackout day
  • Keep guessing — it asks for you instead

Anything with money or a commitment runs through the same checks your dashboard uses, so the assistant cannot promise something your diary can't honour.

17. Frequently asked questions

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. They use normal WhatsApp. That's the whole point.

Can I still reply myself?

Yes, any time. Open the chat and type — the assistant steps back automatically and waits until you hand it back.

Does Bookly hold my money?

No. Payments go directly to your own Paystack account.

Can I use my personal WhatsApp number?

Not if it's currently active on the WhatsApp app. Use a separate business number — it's cleaner anyway.

What happens when my trial ends?

Your account moves to the Free plan (50 messages a month). Your data stays; upgrade whenever you're ready.

Can two people use it at once?

Yes. Add them under Team — subject to your plan's team limit.

Is it only for salons?

No. Anything booked by appointment works — clinics, barbers, tutors, consultants, mechanics, photographers — and shops can take orders instead.
