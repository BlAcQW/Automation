Bookly in one sentence

Bookly is like a receptionist who lives in your WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook Messenger. It answers your clients, books them in and collects the deposit, day and night, even while you're busy with someone.

Why it matters

1

Clients want an answer now

If they wait, they message the next business. Most people book with whoever replies first.

2

You can't always reply

You're with a client, driving, or asleep. Messages at 9 p.m. still deserve an answer.

3

No-shows cost money

An empty slot is income gone. A deposit and a reminder mean people turn up.

What it looks like

Your client chats with your business the way they always do. Bookly replies for you, in seconds, with your real prices and your real free times.

When the deposit is paid, the booking is confirmed and lands in your diary. You didn't have to type a word.

A day with Bookly

One booking, from first message to front door

Here is what happens when a client messages a skin clinic late in the evening.

  • Mon, 9:47 pmEfua sends a WhatsApp message: "Can I get a HydraFacial this week?" The owner is at home with her family.
  • Mon, 9:47 pmBookly replies within seconds with the price and two times that are really free on Thursday.
  • Mon, 9:49 pmEfua picks 4:30 pm. Bookly sends her a payment request for the GH₵ 200 deposit, and she pays by Mobile Money.
  • Mon, 9:50 pmThe booking is confirmed. Efua gets a confirmation. The owner sees it in Bookly, and in Google Calendar if she uses it.
  • Thu, 3:30 pmBookly reminds Efua her appointment is in an hour, with a link to cancel if her plans change, so the slot can go to someone else.
  • Thu, 4:30 pmEfua walks in. The owner never had to stop what she was doing.

And if Efua had asked something Bookly didn't know?

Bookly never guesses. It would tell her someone from the clinic will get back to her, and pass the chat to the owner. The owner can reply herself at any time.

What you get

Everything Bookly does for you

Ten things, in the order you'll notice them.

1

It answers every message

On WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook Messenger, in seconds, at any hour, weekends included. It writes like a friendly member of your staff: short, polite, and in the language your client uses. Regulars are welcomed back by name.

It never makes up a price, a service or a time. It only says what you've set.

What this means for you: no more lost bookings because you replied too late.
2

It knows your business

On a page called Your assistant, you write down what a new receptionist would need to know. There are ten short parts, and every part is optional:

  1. About your business
  2. Your team
  3. Location and parking
  4. Booking rules
  5. Payments and policies
  6. Before and after (how to prepare, aftercare)
  7. Current offers
  8. Questions clients ask
  9. Language and tone
  10. Anything else

Before any client sees it, you can ask Bookly questions yourself and read its answers. If something isn't written down, it doesn't guess: it passes the chat to you.

What this means for you: "Where are you?", "Is there parking?", "What do I do after the peel?" get answered properly, in your words.
3

It books them in properly

Bookly only offers times you're really free. It checks your opening hours, your days off, the appointments you already have, and how many clients you can take at once. It repeats the service, day and time back to the client before booking.

What this means for you: no double bookings, and no "sorry, I'm actually full".
Bookly, explained4
4

It collects the deposit

You choose the deposit for each service. Clients pay by Mobile Money or card on a simple payment page with your business name. The time is held for 30 minutes; if nobody pays, it's freed up for someone else. The booking is only confirmed once the deposit is paid.

Today deposits go through your own Paystack or Payaza account, and we help you set it up. Coming soon Bookly collects for you and you withdraw to your Mobile Money whenever you like, with no account to open.

What this means for you: people who pay a deposit turn up.
5

It reminds your clients

Clients get a confirmation as soon as they book, and a reminder one hour before their appointment, on WhatsApp or by text message. The reminder includes a link to cancel, so if plans change you get the slot back instead of an empty chair. Text messages are sent from "Bookly" on our account: nothing for you to set up or pay separately.

What this means for you: fewer no-shows, and fewer "I forgot".
6

It remembers your regulars

When someone comes back, Bookly greets them by name and knows their past and upcoming appointments. You and your staff can also write a private note on a client, such as "prefers Esi" or "sensitive skin". Bookly uses it quietly to help, and never repeats it to the client.

What this means for you: your regulars feel known, even when you're not the one replying.
7

You're always in charge

Every chat from WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger is in one inbox. You can read what Bookly said, tap Take over to reply yourself, and Hand back when you're done. You get a notification when something needs you.

What this means for you: Bookly does the routine work; you step in when it matters.

8

Your diary and your clients in one place

All bookings in one list you can search and filter, showing who has paid. Bookings can also appear in your Google Calendar automatically. Your client list keeps everyone's history. You manage your services, prices, opening hours and days off from the same place.

What this means for you: no more notebook, and no more "I think I have someone at two".
9

Your team, safely

Give your staff their own logins so they can answer chats and manage bookings. Staff only see the last four digits of a client's number; if they really need the full number they can show it, and that's recorded. You see everything.

What this means for you: your client list stays yours, even if someone leaves.
10

It works on your phone

Use Bookly in your phone's browser or add it to your home screen like an app. Light and dark mode included.

What this means for you: run your bookings from wherever you are.

Questions people ask

Good questions

Do my clients need an app?

No. They use WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger as they already do.

Will it say something wrong?

It only uses your prices, your free times and what you've written in "Your assistant". If it doesn't know, it passes the chat to you.

Can I still reply myself?

Any time. Tap "Take over", reply, then hand the chat back.

Does it sound like a robot?

No. It writes short, friendly messages like your staff would, and you can tell it how to sound.

What if a client cancels?

Their reminder has a cancel link, so the slot frees up for someone else. Your cancellation policy is up to you.

Is my client list safe?

Yes. Staff see only the last four digits of numbers, and every full look-up is recorded. Your private notes are never shown to clients.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use Bookly, and we set it up with you.

Can it handle several clients at once?

Yes. It answers everyone at the same time and tells you how many you can see at once, so it never overbooks.

Why is there a waitlist?

We're letting businesses in a few at a time, so everyone gets a proper start and personal help setting up.

How do I join?

Go to booklygh.com and tap "Join waitlist". We'll message you on WhatsApp or email when it's your turn.
